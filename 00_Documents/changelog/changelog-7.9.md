*Released 2025/09/05*

**JSesh 7.9 is out !**

It features a number of minor improvements, a **lot** of modified signs, a quite a few new texts!

## Fonts

Many signs have been redrawn to fit their Hieroglyphica counterpart or to be more accurate ; A [complete list of changes is available here](/signdiffs/release-7.9).

## Minor improvements

- In the `group` menu, you will find an entry which can vertically center a single sign (basically by adding 0-height spaces around it);

- *explode group* also works on the current selection now;

- *add cartouche* now has a shortcut (control or command C, depending on the platform);

- the hieroglyphic palette won't always be on top.
