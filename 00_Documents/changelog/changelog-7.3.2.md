*Released 2019/04/26*

JSesh 7.3.2 is out !

It's a bug fix to the previous bug fix :-)

Basically, it fixes the possibility to [paste back JSesh pictures **from** word processors](https://jseshdoc.qenherkhopeshef.org/doku.php/doc:en:copy_paste#possibility_to_paste_hieroglyphic_back_text_to_jsesh).

It also includes a couple of fixes on texts from the hieroglyphic text database, and on signs.
