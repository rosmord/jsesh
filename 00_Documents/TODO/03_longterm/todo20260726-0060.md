# Design a tab-stop (tabulation) system

Design a tabulation system:

- horizontal and/or vertical;
- control possibly over the whole page (or a zone, once that works);
- control of the alignment system between two tab stops: space on the
  left, space on the right, or justification;
- keep the "?" behaviour from Winglyph (to be checked);
- otherwise, something along the lines of:
  ~~~
  Code: %[label=1,justification=fill,orientation=h]
  ~~~
  (nice CMYK red for reference: 0 52 62 7)
- by default, the orientation is contextual;
- the label is mandatory;
- we will decide later on the scope of the justifications;
- an EXPLICIT page break certainly clears them;
- `%[clear]` too.
