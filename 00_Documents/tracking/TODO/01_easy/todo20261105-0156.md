# Zone and cartouche "fill"

created
: 20261105

## Need

Suppose a zone in column mode, alone on a line, with horizontal text below it. We want it to fill the whole width of the parent zone, and thus be aligned with the text below it.

## Specification (zones, agreed 2026-11-08)

- Syntax: `%zone[fill=yes]` (`yes`/`no`, like `fitsigns`; a bare `fill` reads as `yes`; `no` is the default and is not written). Edited in the zone properties dialog (*Fill the line*).
- Named by **role**: the zone stretches along the lines of its **parent** — its width if the parent is in lines, its height if it is in columns. (Filling *across* the line, e.g. as tall as the line: not now.)
- It takes **the rest of its line**, as TeX `\hfill`, CSS `flex-grow` or our `HRule`s: alone on its line, the whole length; several fill zones on one line share the free space equally.
- The length of the line: the parent's (page, editor wrapping width, zone `width`; for columns, page height or zone `height`), else the longest line (column) of the parent — as for line alignment.
- Justification: **fill wins**. The line is full, so `justify`, `center` and `end` have nothing left to share (natural gaps). This is what TeX (infinite `fil` glue) and flexbox do.
- Ignored: when the zone has a fixed size along that axis; in a line with `%tab`; when line alignment is ignored (editor *Wrap Lines*, main text only).
- The stretched zone is laid out as if it had the computed length as `width` (or `height`): its lines are aligned in it, its rules span it, nested fill zones fill it. Its other dimension doesn't change.
