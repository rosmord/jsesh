# Zone sizes imposed by the parent

created
: 20261028

Today, a zone has its natural size, or the fixed size given by `width=`/`height=`. Side-by-side registers need the size of a zone to come from its surroundings too:

- stretch to the height of its line (or the width of its column), so that zones side by side have the same height;
- fill the room left in the line (or the parent zone).

Possible syntax: `height=fill`, `width=fill` (the option values may be identifiers). Layout: needs a second pass on the line, once its height is known (as justification does).
