Redefine the different representations of the edited text and their
relationships. In particular, it would be important to specify which
information is kept:

- in the text itself;
- in the part representing the document (with file name, encoding, etc.).

This probably also means moving the knowledge of the document's orientation
into `HieroglyphicText`.
