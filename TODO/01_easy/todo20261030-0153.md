# Fix possible problems in parsing?

created
: 20261030

When asking for a description of the MdC supported by JSesh, I got the following list of problems. 

- tksesh .hie files: `[(A1)]` and `[?-A1-?]` are written back as null-A1-null, which damages the text. LexicalSymbolsUtils has no entry for those two kinds.
- Lost on save: ?, ?? and ^ never reach the model. The options of %quadrat and any unknown %zone/%insert options are also dropped.
- Silent misreadings: <-n-s> loses the sign s, because s> is an end token.
- a#b loses b and switches shading on. **Annoying** ?
- MacScribe .f and !/ parse without error but mean something else.
- Windows line endings: CR LF after a sign counts as a sentence end, not a word end. should not happen.

Small bugs:

- A superscript ending in a backslash doesn't stop at its closing `-`.
- In the line-by-line fallback, philology is always read as signs, even for .hie files.

## Outdated docs (left unchanged):

Choices you may want to check:

Complete cartouches: `<S-, <H-, <F- and <G-` are not listed as obsolete, even though the lexer calls them "old", because MdCModelWriter writes them today. Only the b/m/e part forms are legacy.
- Tab stops and rules: ?N and {lA,B} are listed as legacy because no editor command creates them.
- Legacy origins: where the code doesn't say where a form comes from, the document says "not recorded" rather than guessing. - `\R` direction: "clockwise" comes from reading the Java2D code, not from looking at a rendered sign.
