# Easier editing of transliteration and latin/italic/bold text

created
: 20260915

closed
: 20260926

When clicking in (or moving with the cursors into) transliterations or Latin/italic/bold text, it would be really helpful if i were on a character basis and not just treating the whole text as one object. Having to click the line, then going into the editing bar at the bottom, is more cumbersome than just clicking into the text and editing it inline.  Copying, cutting and pasting parts of the same type of text could also be made easier by doing so inline.

On a side note, I guess joining two or more consecutive +X..+s-parts into one would be nice to.
