A simple demonstration for the use of the Manuel de codage analyser, BZR reader, etc...

The DemoMDCDisplayerAppli class is a very simple, and not too smart displayer for manuel de codage texts. The parser is quite complete, but the displayer knows how to cope with only a small part of the manuel, and will ignore the rest.

A complete viewer can be found in the class jsesh.mdcDisplayer.swing.MDCDisplayerAppli
